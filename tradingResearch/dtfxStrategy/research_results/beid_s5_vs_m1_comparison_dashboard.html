⚠ Key Quantitative Discovery: Why S5 Trading Fails Under Fixed Spread Friction
  • Zone Size vs Spread Fee: On 5-Second bars (S5), the average swing zone is only 2.65 pips (median 1.25 pips, half-height 0.63 pips). With a broker spread of 1.6 pips, the spread cost is 2.5× larger than the entire gross profit target! Every win is converted into a net loss.
  • Transaction Drag: S5 generates 1,535 trades in just 2 weeks ($4.5 imes$ more trades than M1), handing 2,456 pips in pure spread fees to the broker.
  • Microstructure Noise: 5-second swings reflect bid-ask bouncing rather than structural liquidity breaks, dropping the raw target hit rate to 43.97% (vs 67.4% on M1 macro swings).
