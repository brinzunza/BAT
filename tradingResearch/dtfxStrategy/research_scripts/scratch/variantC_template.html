The Longer Reach

What this is (and isn't)

This is the "reach further" target from dtfx.md §7(C), not a validated profitable variant. Same overshoot-then-tag entry gate as Reading the Tape (price must leave the zone behind, then swing back to tag 50%), but the exit target is the last confirmed higher high (buy) / lower low (sell), read once at entry and held fixed. Full-history testing across all four pairs (see the report alongside this chart) found this target genuinely offers a better average reward:risk pre-cost than a plain 1:1R exit — but the realistic 1.6-pip spread this project uses erodes that edge entirely on these mostly-small "every swing" zones, and 1:1R ends up the better performer. This chart is showing the mechanism, not a result to trade.
No spread, slippage, or position sizing is modeled directly on this chart — the sample is one instrument over one very short, recent ~2-day window with no walk-forward or robustness testing beyond what's in the linked report.
