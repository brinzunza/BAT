What this is (and isn't)

This is a backtest replay, not a live feed. The candles are real historical 1-minute OHLC for the week shown; the zones and trades are this version of the rule (every swing a zone, 50% entry, non-inverted direction, 1.6-pip modeled spread, 1-confirmed-position-per-side across all four pairs, margin-checked at 50x leverage) computed after the fact. No slippage, latency, or broker-specific fills are modeled beyond the spread.
This particular week was extremely uneven across pairs, same as every earlier version: USDJPY moved roughly 505 pips top-to-bottom and produced 166 of the week's 201 accepted trades; GBPUSD had 26, EURUSD 9, and EURGBP (only ~59 pips of range) had none. That's a real, honest feature of trading four pairs with one shared rule — some weeks one pair carries the whole book — not a bug in the extraction.
