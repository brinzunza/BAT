How this was built. A hand-designed price path (strictly alternating swings, each one marginally extending the recent same-direction extreme before reversing hard through the opposite one) was fed through the identical dynamic-zigzag pivot detector and pullback-only zone rule used on real price elsewhere this session — then the same touch/entry/stop/target resolver decided every outcome. Three small "confirmation bounce" zones also appear (a genuine, unavoidable side effect of the zigzag needing a real reversal tick to confirm each pivot) — they're tiny and mostly resolve as losses or don't get touched at all, visible if you zoom in, honestly included rather than edited out.
