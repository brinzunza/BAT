Reading the Swing

Your chart, redrawn and labeled — an uptrend of higher highs and higher lows, a break into a downtrend of lower highs and lower lows, then a second break back into an uptrend. Every swing also boxes in a zone: light green between a confirmed high and the higher low under it (buy the dip), light red between a confirmed low and the lower high above it (sell the bounce). Every box now runs forward in time until it's invalidated — price has to leave the zone behind, come back and tag its 50% level (dot on the dashed line), then trade out the far side. Three of the eight zones haven't had that happen yet by the time the chart ends, so their boxes just stay open.
