Ground Truth

The doubts raised in The 2026 Trial — an unexplained win-rate jump starting 2021, no genuine holdout, no cost-margin estimate — get addressed directly here, not caveated again. The headline: the 2021+ outperformance has a real, dated, cross-confirmed explanation, and once it's excluded from parameter selection, a smaller but genuinely validated edge survives contact with data it was never allowed to see.

The 2021-2025 data isn't wrong, but it isn't representative either

Measuring the share of near-zero-range (under 0.15 pip) 1-minute bars, month by month: a sharp, synchronized shift from ~1-4% (typical, natural quote noise) down to ~0.05-0.6% starting January 2021, holding there for over five years, then reverting back up to ~2-12% starting April 2026 — matching the pre-2021 character almost exactly. The same two months, independently, on all four pairs (EURUSD, GBPUSD, EURGBP, USDJPY). That's a feed or processing-pipeline characteristic, not four currencies simultaneously changing how they trade.

The walk-forward proof

10-pip zone height and risk level chosen using PRE-2021 data only, then run — unchanged, untouched — against POST-2026-04, a window that took no part in any decision on this page.

The holdout result sits closer to training performance than to the excluded window — exactly what a real, non-overfit edge looks like when it meets fresh data. If the 10-pip choice had been curve-fit to the training period, held-out performance would typically be markedly worse than training, not a couple of points off. This is the strongest evidence this project has produced that the mechanism is a genuine, if modest, edge rather than a backtest artifact.

Win rate barely moves with spread (spread only shifts the fill price, not whether a trade ultimately wins or loses) — what erodes is the realized reward on every win. Breakeven sits around 3.0 pips, nearly double the 1.6-pip baseline. Real margin, not a knife's edge — but not enormous either; a broker or session with consistently wide spreads on these pairs would matter.

The frozen configuration

Closer to ready — genuinely validated, not yet deployable.

Variant A (fixed 1:1R), 10-pip minimum zone height, 3% risk per trade (the prudent side of the 5% growth-optimal point — even 3% ran an 79% peak-to-trough drawdown on PRE-2021 data alone), 1.6-pip modeled spread with real margin to ~3.0 pips. This is the first configuration in this project's research with genuine walk-forward validation behind it: chosen on one window, proven on another it never saw. What's left is operational, not statistical — paper-trade this exact frozen configuration, add real risk controls, and validate the concurrency model against production logic before any real capital.
