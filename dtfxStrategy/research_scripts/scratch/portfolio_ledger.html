The equity curve

$200 start, 5% of equity risked per trade, capped at 5% of $2,000,000 once equity passes that mark — the same account model this project uses for every growth projection. Log scale: each gridline is 10× the one below it.

This is a mechanical extrapolation of the position-sizing convention, not a claim that $10B is achievable — no real account can risk 5% on ~118 sequential trades a day across four pairs without slippage, execution capacity, correlation, or liquidity limits ever binding. Treat the shape (fast ramp, then sustained linear climb) as the finding, not the dollar figure.

Methodology & caveats

  • Same dynamic-zigzag zone rule as the rest of this research (no fixed lookback, no retracement threshold), pullback + flip zones, 50% entry, symmetric stop/target — traded inverted (fade the pullback), full price history per symbol, 1-minute bars, modeled 1.6-pip spread.
  • The four symbols' resolved trades are merged into one chronological stream, ordered by exit time (when a trade's R is realized), and risked sequentially — this is a combined track record, not a margin-aware simulation of four positions open at once.
  • "Profitable days/weeks/months/years" and the return-distribution stats use the capped-compounding equity curve; a period with zero trades is flat, not profitable.
  • Backtest only: modeled spread cost, no slippage, execution latency, broker-specific fills, or live verification.
